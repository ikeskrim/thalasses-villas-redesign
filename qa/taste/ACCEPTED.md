# Findings reviewed and accepted

`FINDINGS.md` is regenerated by `npm run taste` and reports what it measures,
without exception. This file is the other half: the findings that were looked
at, judged correct, and deliberately left alone.

**The audit is not tuned until it says zero.** A detector adjusted until it
agrees with the page has stopped being an audit, so the reasons live here
instead of in the thresholds.

---

## congestion — the terms page opens with three beats in one viewport

`header.d-pagehead / section.d-terms-notice / section.d-terms`, both widths.

Correct as built. The notice says the terms were inherited from the legacy site,
name another company seven times, and are under legal review. **It has to be
read with the heading, not after a scroll** — a reader who reaches clause 1.0
before learning the document is under review has been misled by the layout.

Direction D's one-idea-per-viewport rule is about pacing a sales narrative. A
legal document is not that, and the exception is deliberate rather than an
oversight the guard happened to catch.

## widow — two litany lines end on a single word at 1440

`The table, set for eighteen.` and `The last of the light, from your own terrace.`

`text-wrap: balance` is applied and doing its job; these are what balance
produces. Both lines are five to nine words in a 590px column at 44px, and there
is no arrangement of them that avoids a short last line — the alternative is
rewriting copy that is correct, or hand-breaking it, which breaks again at the
next viewport and again in Greek.

**Not accepted permanently.** If the litany column widens, these resolve on
their own.

## measure / tracking / collision / off-system — zero, and that is the finding

Every gap on every route at both widths matches a declared rhythm token; no `ch`
measure resolves against the wrong font-size; no two elements in flow overlap;
no small capital is set below +0.02em.

Worth stating explicitly, because three of those four categories were **not**
zero when this audit was first run, and one of them — sixty untracked Clause
tails, on every page of the site — had been shipping since the register was
written.
